What this app is

Keep Muscle is an educational tracking tool for dose, training, protein, and how you feel while using GLP-1 medications. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for care from your physician.

What we collect

In this version, your entries stay on your device. That includes age, goals, training experience, protein target, dose logs, workout checkmarks, protein logs, and check-in ratings. This information is stored in your browser (local storage). It is not sent to Prime Vitality and we do not create an account for you.

What we do not collect

  • No login or password
  • No payment information
  • No location tracking
  • No sale of personal data

Videos and links

Workout videos are hosted with the app so you can see exercise form. Links to Amazon and primevitality.lifestyle open those sites, which have their own policies.

Clearing your data

You can remove app data by clearing this site’s data in your browser or Safari settings. That deletes your logs from that device.
